Privacy Policy for Renee Blog

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Renee Blog ("the app") is a personal desktop tool operated by HU Global Company for managing the operator's own English-language blogs on Blogger. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled. Home page: https://www.huglobals.com/

What the app accesses

When the operator signs in with a Google account, the app asks for one permission: the Blogger scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/blogger). The app uses this permission only to read the operator's own blog information and to create or update draft posts on the operator's own Blogger blogs.

What the app stores

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local private folder. It is never sent to any server operated by us.
  • Draft articles, images and notes are stored in local files on the same computer.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal data from visitors or readers of the blogs through the app.
  • We do not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and no human reads it except the account owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local app folder removes all locally stored data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
